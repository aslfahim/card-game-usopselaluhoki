MYCELIUM WAR

Two fungal colonies. One patch of soil.
Outgrow the rival — spore by spore.

1 · Plant — play spore cards onto your soil or the wild middle row. Each costs nutrients.
2 · Chain — spores beside the same suit grow +1 power each. Linked suits spread fast.
3 · Outgrow — after every round the stronger network drains the weaker colony's life.
☠ Toxin — toxin cards dissolve a rival node instead of planting. ✿ Bloom hits hard but never chains.

First colony to drain the other to 0 life wins. A fungal faction battle — original art, names and rules.
